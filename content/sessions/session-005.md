# Session 5: Enter Stage Left

## The Short Version

The party break Michael out of jail, do some grave robbing and plan to leave Brokensheild.

## What Went Down

I'd been in the prison cell for days, I'd lost track of how many. Those hellspawn threw me in here when I refused to be conscripted. I'd rather die than fight for them, but I still don't understand why they didn't kill me. Why keep me around? Did they have plans for us? I was afraid, but I tried to be strong for the other prison, Father Sequoia, his faith is strong, stronger than mine ever was. He's a good man. 

When I saw my new friends arrive, at first I panicked. There was another hellspawn with them, though this one looked, and acted, differently. It was with [[Doctor Theo]], the mortician Butters and a Dragonkin I didn't recognise. They helped Father Sequoia and I escape. 

After getting me out of prison we got Father Sequoia to safety, helped Waldo find a damage Scythe that was buried in the cemetery then left Waldo to protect Father Sequoia and the others. We were given a note asking us to travel to Derk (Signed by "EH"), but the other's say that's likely a trap given that Derk is largely controlled by the Dark One's army.

With my new friends we made plans for fighting back. At first light we'll travel to Wolden and begin our adventure. If we decide to travel to Derk then we might travel by sea from Wolden to Whitebreak - that's surely safer than going by road.
